# BLT

## Ingredients

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient | Prep |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | lb | bacon | 16 slices, 4 per sandwich |
| 8 | slice | sourdough bread |  |
| 2 | each | tomato | sliced |
| 1 | each | lettuce | a few leaves per sandwich |
| as needed | tbsp | mayonnaise |  |
| to taste | tsp | kosher salt |  |
| to taste | tsp | black pepper |  |

## Instructions

1. Cook the bacon.
2. Toast the bread.
3. Salt and pepper the tomato slices.
4. Spread mayo on the toast and assemble with the bacon, lettuce, and tomato.
