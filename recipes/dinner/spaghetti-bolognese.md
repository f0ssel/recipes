# Spaghetti Bolognese

## Ingredients

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient | Prep |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | lb | ground beef |  |
| 1 | lb | mild italian sausage | ground |
| to taste | tsp | kosher salt |  |
| to taste | tsp | black pepper |  |
| 2 | each | carrot | diced |
| 1/4 | bunch | celery | 2 stalks, diced |
| 1 | each | yellow onion | diced |
| 4 | clove | garlic | minced |
| 1 | can | canned tomato sauce (15 oz) |  |
| to taste | bunch | fresh italian parsley | chopped |
| 16 | oz | spaghetti |  |
| as needed | cup | whole milk | optional |
| to taste | oz | parmesan cheese | grated, for serving |

## Instructions

1. Season the ground beef and sausage with salt and pepper and brown until fond forms on the bottom of the pot.
2. Add the carrot, celery, and onion (not the garlic). Deglaze, then cook until fond forms again.
3. Add the tomato sauce and deglaze.
4. Bring to a boil, then drop to a simmer and cook for 30 minutes.
5. Stir in the Italian parsley and boil the spaghetti.
6. Add a little of the noodle water to the sauce, and milk if you like.
7. Serve over the spaghetti and grate parmesan over each plate.
