# Chicken Soup

## Ingredients

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient | Prep |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | each | whole chicken | split into pieces |
| as needed | tbsp | olive oil | for coating the chicken |
| 4 | each | carrot | cut into large chunks |
| 1/2 | bunch | celery | 4 stalks, cut into large chunks |
| 2 | each | yellow onion | cut into large chunks |
| 6 | clove | garlic |  |
| 1 | bunch | fresh thyme |  |
| 1 | bunch | fresh rosemary |  |
| 1 | each | lemon | juiced |
| to taste | tsp | kosher salt |  |
| to taste | tsp | black pepper |  |

## Instructions

1. Split the whole chicken into pieces and coat them in olive oil.
2. Broil the chicken on the middle rack of the oven until browned. It will still be raw inside.
3. Chop the carrots, celery, and onions into large chunks.
4. Put the browned chicken, vegetables, and herbs in a stock pot and fill with cold water.
5. Bring to a boil, then drop to a simmer and simmer for 1 hour.
6. Remove the chicken and debone it, then add the meat back to the pot.
7. Season with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
