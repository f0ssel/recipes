# Chicken Alfredo

## Ingredients

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient | Prep |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | lb | boneless skinless chicken breast | 2 breasts, flattened |
| to taste | tsp | kosher salt |  |
| to taste | tsp | black pepper |  |
| to taste | tsp | ground nutmeg |  |
| 1 | cup | heavy cream | 8 oz |
| to taste | oz | parmesan cheese | grated, plus more for serving |
| 1 | each | lemon | juiced |
| as needed | tbsp | unsalted butter | for finishing the sauce |
| 8 | oz | spaghetti |  |

## Instructions

1. Flatten the chicken breasts and season with salt and pepper.
2. Sauté the chicken in a pan. Flip when it releases naturally from the pan, cook until done, and set aside.
3. Boil the noodles.
4. Deglaze the fond from the chicken with heavy cream, add the nutmeg, and simmer the cream a few minutes.
5. Remove from heat, add the parmesan, and mix until smooth.
6. Finish the sauce with lemon juice and butter.
7. Add the noodles to the sauce and mix.
8. Slice or chop the chicken into pieces and put it over the pasta.
9. Grate parmesan over the top of each plate.
